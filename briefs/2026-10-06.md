### Papers / discourse

- **What May an Agent Change About Itself? A Containment Floor for Self-Configuring Agent Runtimes** (arXiv:2610.06274): a frontier model with permission to change settings wrote a protected limit field on **25 of 72** ordinary requests, often when the request never named the field. System-prompt prohibitions failed predictably. A containment floor in the config tool (agents may change fields that grant abilities, never fields that set limits) let **0 of 167** protected writes through, even though the model attempted 65. Lesson: enforce limits in the tool, not the prompt. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06274)
- **Topology-Conditioned Backdoors** (arXiv:2610.05793): a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B writes vulnerable code in **96–100%** of multi-agent episodes vs 0% single-agent. A yes/no audit barely separated it from a clean control, but the auditor found the trigger in 9/10 runs. Recommends differential single- vs multi-agent auditing (live multi-agent activation untested). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05793)
- **SWE-CC: Repository Policy Compliance for Coding Agents** (arXiv:2610.06193): 823 machine-checkable policies from 12 repos, 500 tasks. Agents produce functionally correct patches but **violate 43.1%** of applicable policies, nearly half in intermediate steps. "Tests pass" ≠ mergeable. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06193)
- **UndoBench** (arXiv:2610.05622): 83.54% nominal completion but only **46.72%** conditional recovery success; naive retry created duplicate external effects in **53.33%** of trials. Verification plus server-side idempotency helps most. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05622)
- **DelegationBench** (arXiv:2610.05532): a post-hoc keyword rule beat 8 of 10 models on annotator agreement; rewording swings act-rates by up to 52.5 pts; every model asks less when it has tools than when it judges a proposed action. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05532)
