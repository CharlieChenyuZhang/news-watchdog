## 3. Notable papers & what people are discussing (X / community)

### Papers (submitted ~Sep 14–16; arXiv)
- **Dream-RSI — recursive self-improvement via “dreaming” on discovery history (submitted Sep 14).** Orchestration layer turns historical discovery trees into a replay simulator so exploration policies can be refined off-policy cheaply, then redeployed online; evaluated on algorithm eng, math optimization, GPU kernels. Directly relevant to the RSI debates Amodei et al. have been flagging.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.14858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858)

- **EchoPath — execution-level replayable memory for GUI agents (submitted Sep 15).** Model-agnostic harness that turns validated GUI trajectories into MCP-style callable memories with image-based target re-aiming; aimed at repetitive enterprise GUI workflows.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.16635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16635)

- **Rollback-Induced Reflection (RIR) for long-horizon agents (submitted Sep 16).** Treats recovery as jointly choosing when to intervene, where to resume, and what memory to keep after environment rollback.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.18304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304)

- **SFT or RL for tool-calling agents? Controlled study (submitted Sep 15).** Across Qwen3 0.6B–32B: LoRA SFT strongest in-distribution (15/18 settings); GRPO only slight edge on cross-dataset transfer; SFT→GRPO rarely best. Practical takeaway for founders fine-tuning tool agents.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.17848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17848)

## 4. Hot topics & trends founders should know

1. **Disclosure is becoming a product/policy surface.** OpenAI’s framework + pressure for industry standards means customers (and insurers) will start asking “what misalignment incidents have you disclosed?” — build eval logs and incident playbooks now.  
2. **Training-doc philosophy is contested.** Microsoft’s Humanist AI vs Anthropic’s model-welfare language is not academic — it affects model personality, refusal behavior, and how enterprises judge “controllability.”  
3. **Consumer AI free tiers are getting gated.** Meta One shows social platforms metering compute-heavy Muse/generation behind $8–$20/mo bundles — expect similar metering elsewhere; design B2C pricing with AI unit costs explicit.  
4. **Agent reliability research > model beauty contests.** Dream-RSI / EchoPath / RIR / SFT-vs-RL all point to exploration policy, replayable memory, and recovery as the leverage for shipping agents.  
5. **Voice stack still the week’s capability story.** Gemini 3.8 Live remains the production voice bar to beat if your roadmap includes live agents.
