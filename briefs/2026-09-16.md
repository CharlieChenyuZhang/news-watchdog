## 3. Notable papers & what people are discussing (X / community)

### Papers (last ~1–2 weeks; arXiv)
- **State of Thought (SoT) — endogenous reasoning via internal state (submitted Sep 13).** Compact dynamics-geometric state + tiny controller; reported accuracy gains with **~62.6% fewer tokens** and **~44.6% lower latency** across many tasks/models.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.16055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16055)

- **Thinking with Looped Flows (submitted Sep 10).** Trains looped models with local denoising objectives so early updates support later ones; **58.8% on ARC-AGI-1**, **12.2% on ARC-AGI-2**.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.11801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11801)

- **PARSER — parallel read / deep reason for long-context agents (submitted Sep 6).** Scatter–gather over chunk subagents; +5.7 pts avg vs strongest sequential memory baseline, up to **11× lower latency**, strong at 896K tokens.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.06702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06702)

- **Procedural Graphs for self-evolving agent execution (submitted Sep 8).** Procedure–relation–procedure graphs + self-edit loop from failed vs successful trajectories.  
  Source: [arXiv:2609.09153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09153)

## 4. Hot topics & trends founders should know

1. **Industry self-regulation is moving from essays → meetings.** OpenAI/Anthropic/Google safety talks + embedded-evaluator commitments are the near-term compliance/reputation story. Expect more disclosure requirements, third-party eval access, and possible antitrust-waiver politics.  
2. **Voice agents hit production-grade.** Same week: Gemini 3.8 Live (Google), GPT‑Live‑1 (OpenAI API), MAI‑Transcribe‑2 (Microsoft) — voice UX + STT cost/latency are compressing fast. If your product still uses cascaded STT→LLM→TTS only, re-evaluate.  
3. **Agent harnesses > raw model swaps.** OpenAI Agents API (Codex harness), Meta Muse Code, Google Live API partner stack (LiveKit, Pipecat, Vercel, etc.) — differentiation is shifting to orchestration, sandboxes, evals, and tool governance.  
4. **Pacing vs China / IPO tension.** Amodei explicitly frames pacing as limited by the US lead over China; Reuters notes OpenAI & Anthropic IPO prep as a counter-incentive to slow. Founders should watch for compute/export and “frontier definition” rules.  
5. **Efficiency research is hot.** SoT / PARSER / latent CoT papers mirror the product race: same quality, fewer tokens, lower latency — unit economics for agent products.
