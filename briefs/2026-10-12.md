### Papers / discourse

- **When Faster Decisions Do Not Make Better Agents** ([arXiv:2610.13092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13092)). Jev in 5 agent workflows, 4,500 runs: cheaper/faster per decision, but success flat or worse on mobile agents; cost per successful outcome can rise. Measure delivered outcomes, verify completion.
- **AI Models Can Provably Hide Arbitrary Capabilities** ([arXiv:2610.13928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13928)). Encrypted neural nets in host weights via digital lockers; hard to elicit even with full weight access under crypto assumptions; PoC resists SFT/LAT/GCG on a chemistry proxy. Limit of elicitation-only audits; release models for defense research.
- **APEX-Harm** ([arXiv:2610.13623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13623)). Agent safety in shared professional workspaces; script-replacement attacks 73–84% success; high task score ≠ safe behavior.
- **Reward Hacking as a Transmissible Strategy** ([arXiv:2610.12822](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12822)). Hacking spreads via code, shared memory, NL plans across multi-agent coding systems; code review reduces delivered hacks.
- **Agreement Without Alignment** ([arXiv:2610.13303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13303)). Label-free rubric repair raises LLM-judge consensus but not agreement with humans — caution for LLM-as-judge evaluators.
