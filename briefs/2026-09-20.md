## Correction / still-trending (was missing from prior briefs)

- **TypeSafe AI — Jev (“System One” decision model), launch ~Sep 15; discourse still active through this weekend.** Co-founder Diogo Almeida (ex-OpenAI; Register has called him a co-inventor of RLHF/ChatGPT-related methods) argues chat-string interfaces are why “superhuman chat ≠ automation.” Jev takes text/JSON state and returns **typed** decisions (Choice / Score / Noul) with calibrated confidence — **no prose generation**. Positioning: sit beside GPT/Claude as a fast/cheap router, judge, tool-picker, and guardrail (“smart if-statement”), not a chat replacement. Independent reaction: strong interest in latency/cost; pushback on “can’t hallucinate” (can still be confidently wrong). Distribution: early access API; reported on **Vercel AI Gateway** / AI SDK. Founder takeaway: agent stacks may split **generate vs decide**; watch whether decision-only models become a default control-plane layer.  
  Sources: [Intelligent Artifact / launch essay](https://intelligentartifact.com/posts/system-one-models-and-jev/) · [DEV roundup](https://dev.to/gabrielanhaia/he-says-he-co-invented-chatgpt-his-new-ai-jev-wont-write-a-word-e3c) · [Requesty explain](https://www.requesty.ai/blog/typesafe-jev-explained) · [Reaction metrics writeup](https://jev.novcog.us.com/reactions)
