**Founder take (delta vs Oct 4):** Two real shifts. First, local got practical: a 125B-class model on a gaming GPU behind an OpenAI-compatible localhost API is now the top AI thread, so if inference cost or data privacy is in your pitch, benchmark Strata/Qwen on your workload this week (quality caveats are real). Second, safety stopped being one resignation and became a policy, legal and liability story: Washington put a national-security body on it, OpenAI's CEO is publicly arguing for tolerated harm, and lawsuits are reportedly piling up — expect enterprise buyers to ask harder questions about agent controls. Builder tools plateaued; the useful lessons are ops (cap spend, write docs, sandbox properly). On decision models, run Jev/Clef against a small classifier and an LLM judge on *your* data before committing. Decisions API, Argon/Fairwind and Clef RL are all **still gated**.
