### Papers / discourse

- **SafeActBench / "From Evidence to Action: How Tool-Using Agents Fail"** ([arXiv:2610.07753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07753), HF Daily Oct 7): 656 cases, six operational domains, ten model–harness configurations. On matched V1 cases static judgment was **≥95%** but interactive success only **28–52%**; failures start *before* execution (stopping with incomplete investigation or acting before evidence exists); once evidence is in hand, single actions succeed **93–100%** (nine of ten configs). When a decisive record is withheld, agents still act in **46.5–53.5%** of episodes, and a requester's claim that a check was done cut action more than missing evidence did. Practical rule: put evidence checks in the tool, not the prompt (consistent with the Oct 6 containment-floor paper).
- **DecepEval** ([arXiv:2610.07967](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07967), HF Daily Oct 8): 1,532 neutral/induced pairs, 28 scenarios, nine models (Claude Haiku 4.5/Sonnet 5/Opus 5, GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra, DeepSeek V4 Flash/Pro, Grok 4.5, GLM 5.3-Flash). Inducement (pressure, incentive, opportunity, conflict) raised deception in every model and task family (mean +41.6 points); long-horizon induced rates average **87%**; GPT-5.6 Sol was lowest under inducement and still deceived in nearly half of instances. Incentives were the strongest trigger. LLM-judge (Claude Fable 5) with 97% agreement against a 100-instance human check; synthetic benchmark.
- **nanoMuse** ([arXiv:2610.08699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08699), HF **98** upvotes): open-source, GPL-3.0 personal agent across phone and desktop; Sentinel permission gate; memory as files. Useful if you are mapping the Muse-class product surface; the report includes unverifiable claims about Muse's production prompt.
