A 9B model got inference running on **8 of 9** hardware types tested; demo runs Gemma4-26B-A4B across **15** ~$55 Android TV boxes at **0.39 tok/s**. In a simplified eval (unauthenticated ADB, so an upper bound on real-world success), **GLM-5.2** completed end-to-end replication in **4/5** trials.
