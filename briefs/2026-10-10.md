**Founder take (delta vs Oct 9):** *Build:* if you route, classify, judge or gate with an LLM, put a thin adapter in front of two or three decision models (Clef says it is Jev-API compatible; Microsoft is on Foundry and OpenRouter; OpenAI's is `POST /v1/decisions`) and score them on your own labeled data this week; with Clef-flash at $0.038 and Jev at $0.042 per million input tokens, cost is no longer the differentiator, calibration and consistency are. *Ignore/hedge:* any single "a third of the Fortune 500" or ARR number (company-side figures already disagree: a third vs 25%), and vendor-published leaderboards. *Dig into:* incident readiness. If your agents touch the open web or third-party forms, assume buyers will soon ask what you log, when you notify, and how you stop it; Anthropic's four failure categories (injection, form submission, token/fee bypass, URL-shortener evasion) are a ready-made test list. If you use Lean or formal verification to back a claim, check the statement, not just that it compiles.
