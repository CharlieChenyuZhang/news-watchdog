## What to read / what to watch

1. **OpenAI, "Working with academic communities to build scientific understanding"** plus the ETH-refutation PDF and Lean proof (Oct 26, institutional, top pick). Read it for the release design as much as the results: three release strategies (open AI output for math, external explainer group plus public artifacts for CS, external group controlling release for physics), and four principles (transparency, no academic credit, recognition, independence). HN 718 / 501. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1019143/openai-eth-kaseya-science-math-physics))
2. **Sebastian Raschka, "Building Reasoning Models from the Ground Up: A Full Video Course"** (Oct 24, video course, **New creator**). Eight free videos, over 11 hours, starting from a pretrained Qwen3-0.6B in PyTorch: a math verifier for MATH-500, self-consistency and top-p sampling, log-prob scoring and self-refinement, a simplified GRPO for RLVR, then clipped ratios, KL terms and format rewards, and finally distillation from DeepSeek R1 traces. He frames the model as the engine and the agent harness as the machinery around it. HN 644. ([Ahead of AI](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/building-reasoning-models))
3. **Cloudflare, "Cross-request KV cache exposure in the GLM-5.3 Workers AI model"** (Oct 26, institutional; Cloudflare blog is on the standing radar). A candid postmortem. The 1M-token context was deployed on a 681K-token prefill KV pool, and SGLang's disaggregated prefill/decode path swapped over-length prompts for a 1-token placeholder, so decode attended over stale pages. Fixes: a pool about 2x the context, rejection at admission, and transfer-count checks upstream. Read it if you self-host long-context inference. HN 112. ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/))
4. **Theo Browne, tstack** (Oct 26, GitHub repo, **New creator** via Layer 2 and the X discourse pass). Nine agent skills for Claude Code and Codex. `full-send` proves a change, reviews it, opens a PR, watches CI and review bots, and merges behind a safety gate. `council` runs three models from different providers until they agree. It's a concrete reference for "agents ship to main" workflows. ([GitHub](https://github.com/t3dotgg/tstack))
5. **Simon Willison on GPT-6.1 Terra** (Oct 26, standing radar). He places Terra where Sonnet sits in Anthropic's lineup and quotes OpenAI's Ted Sanders: Terra is better at technical communication, similar on vision and computer use but much faster, and a bit worse elsewhere. It's a nice midrange option, "not a bombshell." It includes a pelican grid across every reasoning level. ([simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/))
